Privacy Policy — The Mercer Files Uploader

Last updated: November 3, 2026

The Mercer Files Uploader is an internal tool of Global Export Import Group. It is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Mercer Files to manage that one channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails, titles and descriptions to The Mercer Files channel and set their publishing time;
  • read basic information about that channel (to confirm it uploads to the right channel);
  • read that channel's own statistics (views, watch time, subscribers, audience retention, traffic sources) to prepare reports for the channel owner.

The app does not access other people's channels, personal Google account data, email, contacts or files.

How the data is used and stored

Channel statistics are used only to prepare reports for the channel owner. The access token and the reports are stored on the owner's private computer. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, stored tokens stop working, and stored reports are deleted on request.
